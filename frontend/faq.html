How do I request a room?

Open Book a Room, select a proposed room and time, then enter your name, UH email, and purpose. The form validates your information before showing a demo message.

Does submitting confirm my booking?

No. This phase demonstrates the front-end request flow. Live availability and confirmation require the backend, which will be added later.

Which rooms can I request?

The form shows five proposed types: Collaboration, Study, Multi-purpose, Quiet Study, and Meeting. Confirm final room details against the course specifications.

What email should I enter?

Enter a valid UH address ending in @cougarnet.uh.edu or @uh.edu.

How long can I book a room for?

The project plan does not specify a booking limit. The form checks that the end time is later than the start time. Final scheduling rules belong in the backend phase.

Where can I find official hours and location?

Official hours and a street address were not included in the Phase 1 plan. Check the UH Sugar Land website.
